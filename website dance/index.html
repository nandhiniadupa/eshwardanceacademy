ABOUT THE ACADEMY

A SPACE TO LEARN.
A STAGE TO SHINE.

Every dancer starts somewhere. Take your first step with Eshwar Dance Academy.

We welcome learners who want to discover dance, improve their skills, build confidence or prepare for a special performance. With 15+ years of experience, the academy also offers choreography and event-related services for celebrations and programmes.

Whether you are exploring a new dance style, preparing for a wedding, or organising a college or family event, contact us to discuss your requirements and available timings.
